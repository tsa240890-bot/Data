Why Studying Real Companies Makes Theory Click

Published 12 June 2026

Textbook definitions of concepts like "economies of scale" feel abstract until you see them in action. When Amazon negotiates bulk shipping rates or Netflix spreads content costs over millions of subscribers, you're watching the same principle from your syllabus play out in real time. Try picking one company you know well and mapping each chapter's concept onto its actual operations — it turns memorisation into understanding.

The 4 Ps Aren't Just for Exams

Published 2 May 2026

Product, Price, Place, and Promotion show up everywhere — including in how your favourite snack brand decided on its packaging, or why a new phone launches at a premium price before dropping. Next time you see an ad or notice a product on a shelf, ask: which of the 4 Ps is this decision really about?

Reading a Balance Sheet Without Fear

Published 18 April 2026

A balance sheet is just a snapshot: what a business owns (assets), what it owes (liabilities), and what's left for the owners (equity). The golden rule — Assets = Liabilities + Equity — always balances. Once that clicks, ratio analysis and financial statements stop feeling like a foreign language.
