1. Nature and Significance of Management

Management is the process of getting things done with the aim of achieving goals effectively and efficiently. It is multidimensional — involving management of work, people, and operations.

  • Functions: Planning, Organising, Staffing, Directing, Controlling.
  • Characteristics: Goal-oriented, pervasive, multidimensional, continuous, group activity, dynamic, intangible force.
2. Principles of Management

Fayol's 14 principles include Division of Work, Authority & Responsibility, Discipline, Unity of Command, and Esprit de Corps.

Taylor's Scientific Management focuses on Science (not rule of thumb), Harmony, Cooperation, and Development of workers to their fullest potential.

3. Business Environment

The business environment consists of all external factors (economic, social, political, technological, legal) that influence business decisions.

  • Economic environment: Inflation, interest rates, income levels.
  • Social environment: Customs, traditions, values, social trends.
  • Technological environment: Innovations affecting product/process design.
4. Marketing Management

Marketing mix = Product, Price, Place, Promotion (the 4 Ps).

  • Product: Branding, packaging, labelling, product mix.
  • Price: Pricing objectives and strategies (skimming, penetration).
  • Place: Channels of distribution.
  • Promotion: Advertising, personal selling, sales promotion, publicity.
5. Financial Management

Financial management deals with planning, organising, and controlling financial activities.

  • Financial decisions: Investment, Financing, Dividend decisions.
  • Sources of finance: Equity shares, debentures, retained earnings, trade credit, bank loans.
  • Financial statements: Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit & Loss.

7. Entrepreneurship Development

Entrepreneurship involves identifying opportunities, organising resources, and bearing risk to start a venture.

  • Process: Idea generation, feasibility study, business plan, launch.
  • Qualities: Risk-taking, innovation, resilience, vision.
